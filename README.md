During the duration of my course on Technical Writing, I have learnt the skills of communicating information, organizing professional documents, conducting research, and explaining technical information.

In this portfolio, there are five assignments which showcase the skills I have acquired during this course.

## Portfolio Projects

1. Professional Email Writing
Through this task, I gained an appreciation for the significance of writing professional emails within the workplace environment. I got to know the correct way of organizing information and communicating with people.

2. Orange Production Chart
This task required me to construct a table and chart in Excel for comparing the production of oranges by various countries from 2017 to 2021. Through this task, I have enhanced my skill of organizing data and presenting it visually and in a meaningful way.

3. Step-by-Step Technical Instructions 
How to Turn an iPhone Into a Mobile Hotspot and Connect a Laptop
For this assignment, I provided step by step directions for a person to use a mobile hotspot on their iPhone to get their laptop online. Also, I used a flow chart to make things easier to understand. This assignment helped me realize the importance of communicating technical information in easy-to-understand language.

4. Cybersecurity Training Proposal
The objective of this task was to design a proposal for employee cybersecurity training. I highlighted the importance of cybersecurity awareness and gave recommendations about how companies could help their employees identify cyber threats. This project has enhanced my research and problem-solving skills.

5. Research Report 
The Future of Work: Remote vs. In-Office
In the current assignment, I studied the pros and cons of telecommuting against working from the office. In doing so, I referred to various sources in order to back up my findings. In addition, I arranged my information into a professional report.
